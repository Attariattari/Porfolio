# Muhyo Tech Off-Page Authority Playbook

## Objective

Build genuine topical and brand authority for Muhyo Tech through relevant editorial links, business citations, partnerships, and expert contributions. Domain-rating metrics are useful directional indicators, but the primary success signals are qualified referring domains, non-branded organic visibility, referral traffic, and leads.

## Non-Negotiable Quality Rules

- Do not buy bulk backlink packages or use private blog networks.
- Do not automate unsolicited mass outreach.
- Do not use exact-match commercial anchor text repeatedly.
- Do not place links on unrelated, unmoderated, or obviously generated pages.
- Prefer a small number of relevant editorial links over a large number of low-quality links.
- Every claim used in outreach must be verifiable on a public Muhyo Tech page.

## Link Acquisition Priorities

### 1. Trusted business citations

Create and fully complete profiles on legitimate platforms relevant to a Pakistan-based software business. Keep the business name, website URL, founder name, location, service description, logo, and social profiles consistent.

Suitable categories include:

- Google Business Profile, where eligibility requirements are met.
- LinkedIn company and founder profiles.
- GitHub organization or founder profile.
- Reputable Pakistan business and technology directories.
- Relevant local chambers, incubators, coworking communities, and startup directories.
- Carefully reviewed agency directories that provide a real editorial or verification process.

### 2. Project and technology citations

- Publish useful public repositories, starter kits, or small developer tools on GitHub.
- Add an accurate website link and project documentation to each relevant repository.
- Submit genuinely useful packages or templates to appropriate ecosystem directories.
- Turn completed client work into permission-based case studies that clients can reference from their own sites.

### 3. Partner and client links

- Request a factual vendor or technology-partner mention after successful delivery.
- Offer clients a concise project summary they can publish on their news or partner page.
- Build reciprocal value through integrations, joint case studies, interviews, and technical implementation notes—not direct link swaps.

### 4. Expert contributions and digital PR

- Pitch practical engineering lessons to reputable development, startup, SaaS, and Pakistan business publications.
- Contribute expert quotes through journalist and founder communities.
- Publish original benchmarks, teardown studies, checklists, and implementation data that writers can cite.
- Repurpose strong Muhyo Tech research into LinkedIn posts and GitHub resources that link back to the canonical article.

## Link-Worthy Content Assets

Prioritize assets with original utility rather than generic opinion:

1. Pakistan website-development cost benchmark with transparent scope assumptions.
2. Next.js performance benchmark using reproducible test methodology.
3. Admin-dashboard security and launch checklist.
4. MERN application architecture decision guide.
5. Core Web Vitals audit template for small businesses.
6. Open-source project-planning or SEO validation utility.
7. Detailed client case study with measurable before-and-after outcomes.

Each asset should include a clear author, last-updated date, methodology, evidence, downloadable or reusable material, and links to relevant service and project pages.

## 90-Day Execution Cadence

### Days 1–30: Entity consistency and foundations

- Audit all existing profiles for consistent business and founder information.
- Complete the highest-trust business and professional profiles.
- Prepare one original link-worthy asset.
- Identify 40 relevant publications, communities, partners, directories, and podcasts.
- Record the current referring-domain baseline in Google Search Console and a backlink analysis platform.

### Days 31–60: Outreach and partnerships

- Send five carefully personalized outreach messages per week.
- Pitch two expert contributions or interviews per week.
- Ask eligible clients or partners for factual project citations.
- Publish and distribute the first link-worthy asset.
- Convert useful technical work into one public GitHub resource.

### Days 61–90: Repeat what earns results

- Publish the second research, tool, or case-study asset.
- Follow up once on relevant unanswered outreach.
- Expand the channels that generated qualified links or referral visits.
- Reclaim unlinked brand mentions and fix broken inbound links.
- Review anchor diversity, link relevance, indexed referring pages, and lead quality.

## Prospect Qualification Checklist

Before outreach, confirm that the prospect:

- Is topically relevant to software, business, startups, web development, or the local market.
- Publishes real editorial content with identifiable authors or owners.
- Has indexed pages and a genuine audience.
- Does not openly sell large volumes of followed links.
- Can provide contextual value beyond a directory-style profile link.
- Is safe and appropriate for the Muhyo Tech brand.

## Tracking Fields

Track every prospect and acquired mention using:

- Prospect/domain
- Category and relevance
- Contact person
- Target page
- Proposed value or contribution
- Outreach date
- Follow-up date
- Status
- Live referring URL
- Anchor or mention text
- Follow/nofollow/sponsored attribute
- Indexed status
- Referral visits
- Leads or conversions
- Notes

## Monthly KPIs

- New qualified referring domains
- Links from topically relevant pages
- Indexed referring pages
- Branded-search impressions
- Non-branded commercial-query impressions
- Referral traffic and engaged sessions
- Leads attributed to referrals or organic search
- Lost links reclaimed
- Original assets published

Avoid setting a target for raw backlink count. A sustainable initial target is two to five genuinely relevant referring domains per month, with quality and business relevance reviewed manually.
